About Us

A small cafe with a big love for coffee.

Our Story

Brew & Bean started in 2020 as a tiny corner stall with one espresso machine and a big dream: to make great coffee feel friendly and affordable.

Today we serve thousands of cups every month, but we still roast in small batches, bake fresh every morning and greet every guest by name.
